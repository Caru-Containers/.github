## 🚢 About CARU Containers

We are a global container trader with Dutch roots. From our headquarters in Rotterdam and offices in 10 countries, we buy, sell, rent and lease new and used shipping containers in more than 70 countries worldwide. Our customers use them for cargo-worthy transport, safe storage, offshore operations (DNV 2.7-1) and purpose-built modifications — anything from a site office to a pop-up bar.

Founded in 2000 from the merger of CATU Rotterdam, Trade Craft and Lease Craft, CARU set out to make container trading more professional and more human. That still drives us today: speed and certainty in every deal, transparent pricing, and a team that picks up the phone.

We believe the container is the greatest invention of the 20th century. It's an unpretentious product — it does what it does, and it does it well. And because it's so simple, the possibilities are endless.

## 💻 Why we're on GitHub

Just like our containers, we're always on the move — exploring new markets, new ways of trading and new digital technologies. We launched the first fully automated container webshop in the Benelux, and we keep building the tools that make buying, renting and leasing containers easy, accessible and transparent.

This organization is where our engineering team collaborates on:

- 🛒 **E-commerce & webshops** — self-service container ordering across our markets
- 📦 **Inventory & depot tooling** — real-time stock, depot and logistics integrations
- 🔌 **APIs & integrations** — connecting CARU systems with partners, carriers and ERPs
- 🧰 **Internal tools & automation** — the boring-but-essential stuff that keeps a global trader running
- 🌱 **Open source** — libraries and utilities we're happy to share with the community

Public repositories are open for issues and pull requests. Internal projects live in private repositories.

## 🌍 Sustainability

A container that gets reused is a container that doesn't need to be built. We keep movements as local as possible and sell, lease or rent every container for a full purpose — to use, re-use and repair. Less waste of all kinds, and a fair price.
